# My Appliance Repair SEO and GEO audit

November 15, 2026 · Production `www.myappliance.us` · Connecticut and Bergen County, New Jersey

## Why Google is slow to index the site

**Google had discovered most of the submitted pages by September but had deferred crawling them.** The September Search Console screenshots showed 62 URLs in **Discovered – currently not indexed**, four in **Crawled – currently not indexed**, three alternate canonicals, and five indexed. The sitemap report showed 73 discovered pages. Crawl Stats showed healthy hosts, 97% HTTP 200 responses, and 4% of requests classified as discovery. Those are historical figures. The current Search Console session required sign-in, and the repository’s performance export is filtered to the homepage for May 31–June 6. [September inspection and Crawl Stats evidence](seo-audit-2026-09-17.md) · [Export filters](../latest-google-performance/Filters.csv) · [Google’s indexing status definitions](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7440203)

**Today’s live crawl found no sitewide indexing block.** All 81 submitted URLs work, allow indexing, declare matching canonicals, and are reachable within two clicks. Seven representative Googlebot-smartphone user-agent HTML requests and both discovery files worked. The leading explanation for the recorded September backlog is limited first-time crawl demand. The fast expansion of the article inventory, weaknesses in original content evidence, and sparse verified branch-specific corroboration give Google fewer reasons to prioritize these URLs. How much each factor contributed is unknown. A URL Google has not crawled has no recorded page-level quality decision. [Google on crawl demand](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawl-budget) · [Google on crawling and indexing](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/how-search-works)

The publish history supports the timing. The September audit records a jump in discovered URLs in early August. The current site retains 17 posts dated July or August, and Git records repeated two-post publishing runs in early August. Ten Bergen town pages were introduced in the September rollout, and four Hartford-area city pages were added November 11. The current inventory is 81, up from 73 in the September production snapshot. A comparison with the two other websites would require their publication dates, signals of discovery, and Search Console inspection histories. 2. **Fix factual issues and improve five landing pages.** Start with Hamden, New Haven, washer repair, refrigerator repair and the Bergen hub. Verify coverage, branch identity and job records.
