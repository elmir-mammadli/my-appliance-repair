# SEO audit follow-up — 2026-11-11

Health score at audit time: about 71/100. Technical 84, content 62, on-page 66,
schema 66, AI readiness 68, local 52, search experience 58. The site is technically
solid; what held it back was conflicting facts, claims with nothing behind them, and
very little outside proof (listings, reviews, links).

## Fixed in code (this change)

### Critical
- **llms.txt** no longer a hand-written file. `src/app/llms.txt/route.ts` renders it from
  `src/lib/business.ts`, `services`, `cities`, `branches` and `posts`, so the hours,
  ratings, warranty, "insured" (never "licensed"), the NJ branch and the service list
  can't drift from the pages again. The stale `public/llms.txt` is deleted (it would
  have shadowed the route).

### High
- **Warranty** reads "90-day parts and labor warranty" everywhere (hero ×2, Bergen page,
  Bergen metadata, WhyUs process step, city CTA).
- **Diagnostic** is never "free". WhyUs copy and the booking confirmation email now say
  the $99 service call is waived when you go ahead with the repair.
- **Hours** claim in WhyUs now matches the posted 8am–7pm, 7 days, plus the evening
  emergency line.
- **"Connecticut's most trusted"** removed (about page schema, blog CTA). Replaced with
  the rating the site can back: 5.0 on Google and Thumbtack.
- **"N communities"** now counts towns only; "Hartford County" no longer counts as a
  community (`SERVICE_AREA_COUNT`).
- **Structured data** has one business node (`HomeAndConstructionBusiness`,
  `@id https://www.myappliance.us/#business`) rendered by the footer. City pages emit a
  `Service` for that city with `provider` → the business, instead of a second business
  node. Service pages and the about page reference it by `@id`. The NJ branch is its own
  node with the correct name, `parentOrganization` → CT, and the CT node lists it as a
  `subOrganization`.
- **Profile links** come from `PROFILES` in `business.ts`. `sameAs` now uses the real Yelp
  business page, not the "write a review" page, and includes Facebook (the page loads;
  the audit's error was a bot block). Thumbtack count updated to 182.
- **Reviews on city pages**: a "5.0 ★ on Google & Thumbtack" chip in the hero trust row.

### Medium
- Security headers: nosniff, Referrer-Policy, X-Frame-Options, CSP frame-ancestors,
  Permissions-Policy.
- `images.minimumCacheTTL` = 31 days.
- Hero image preload deduped (both breakpoints share one `sizes`, so React emits one
  `<link rel=preload>`).
- BookingModal and HiringModal load on first open via `LazyBookingModal` /
  `LazyHiringModal` (the booking chunk is also warmed on idle so the first click is
  instant). The map was already dynamic.
- Sitemap: every URL has `lastModified`. Blog posts use `updatedAt ?? date`; page
  templates use the `CONTENT_UPDATED` map in `sitemap.ts` — bump it when that page
  type's content changes. `/privacy` and `/terms` are noindex and out of the sitemap.
- Titles under 60 characters: root default, homepage, city pages, about, booking.
  City descriptions name each city's own neighborhoods.
- Blog `Article.author` now matches the byline (Person for named technicians,
  Organization for the team). Team byline casing normalised in `posts.json`.

### Low
- `/new-jersey` → `/new-jersey/bergen-county` (301).
- Homepage has one `<h1>`; the desktop heading is `role="heading" aria-level=1`.
- City pages: "Air Conditioner Repair" (no service page) replaced by the
  freezer/microwave/cooktop service linking to `/services/more-appliances`.
- Footer "Same-Day Emergency" now points at the emergency section (`/#emergency`).
- IndexNow was already wired (`scripts/indexnow.mjs`, key file in `public/`); run it
  after deploy.

## Owner decisions applied on 2026-11-11

- **Testimonials**: the 20 in-house quotes (which were not even rendered; the section
  showed a Housecall Pro iframe) are replaced by 24 verbatim excerpts from the Google
  profile in `src/lib/reviews.ts`, each with the reviewer's display name, the month and
  "Google" as source. The iframe is gone; the marquee renders the cards. Google count is
  now 109 (profile, 2026-11-11). To add a review, copy it from the profile verbatim.
- **Years of experience**: "6+ years" everywhere. The microwave post's "20 years" line
  now reads "six-plus years of appliance repair across Connecticut".
- **Hartford County**: four city pages added in `src/lib/cities.ts` (Hartford, West
  Hartford, New Britain, Newington; region "Greater Hartford", so they cross-link as
  nearby). Farmington, Plainville, Berlin and Middletown stay in the service list without
  pages; add them the same way if wanted.
- **NJ town pages**: `BergenTown` now carries a tagline, two paragraphs about the
  town's housing and the repairs that follow from it, a neighborhood list, a town-specific
  preparation note and three town-specific FAQs. `BergenPage` renders all of it and the
  metadata description/keywords come from the town data, so no two pages share a
  template sentence.
- **Playwright SEO suite** runs again: two stale assertions fixed (the inline form's
  success heading and the sitemap lastmod count, which the blog index now shares).

## Not changed — needs the owner

- **Emergency line in business hours**: still kept out of `openingHoursSpecification`
  on purpose so the Google Business Profile hours stay unchanged.
- **Price ranges per appliance** for the cost FAQ (AI answers currently quote
  competitors). Needs real numbers.
- **Off-site**: add the website URL to Thumbtack, Yelp, Facebook and the Google Business
  Profile; link the BBB listing; claim Angi, Nextdoor, Bing Places, Apple Business
  Connect. If the Hamden address is residential, set GBP to service-area mode.
- **http → www** takes two redirect hops. The first hop is Vercel's own http→https
  upgrade and cannot be removed in the dashboard; the apex→www redirect is already
  permanent. Harmless; close this item.
- `/booking` was left indexable on purpose; it is a legitimate landing page.

## Not covered by the audit

Real speed measurements (PageSpeed API refused), Google Maps rankings, Search Console
data.
