## Reading

The site is sound; authority is the constraint. Six external links cannot earn crawl for
81 URLs. On-page changes will not move indexing much until listings, links and reviews grow.

## Changed in this pass

- `/bookings` permanently redirects to `/booking`.
- `/booking` and the New Jersey booking form are `noindex, follow` and out of the sitemap.
- `REVIEW_RATINGS` refreshed to the 2026-11-25 profile counts.
- "MyAppliance" spelling replaced with the canonical name in public HTML.
- `keywords` metadata removed everywhere.
- `/about` has its own Open Graph and Twitter tags and a description under 160 characters.
- The 404 title no longer repeats the brand.
- `alternateName` added to the business node.

## Not changed, needs the owner

- Off-site: listings (Angi, HomeAdvisor, BBB, Apple, Bing Places), local links, review flow.
- Ask Meridian Web Systems to remove the demo host from Google.
- `WhyUs` and `AboutContent` say "Thumbtack Platinum"; the public profile shows "Top Pro".
- `PROFILES.google` is a `share.google` short link; the full Maps URL is a better `sameAs`.
- `geo`, `foundingDate` and `founder` for the business node.
