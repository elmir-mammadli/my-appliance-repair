## Executive summary

**What works.** No crawl or index blocks anywhere: robots allows everything but `/api/`, every sitemap URL returns 200 with `index, follow`, one matching canonical, one H1, a unique title and description, parseable JSON-LD, and zero images without alt. Apex, http, trailing-slash and legacy URLs all 308 to the canonical in one hop. Security headers, HSTS, AVIF images, consent-gated GA4, generated `llms.txt`, IndexNow key and RSS are all live. Lab performance is strong: service and city pages score 98–100 on mobile, desktop home is 100. The Google Business Profile ranks in the local 3-pack for both Hamden (#2) and New Haven (#3) with 110 five-star reviews and working Website and Schedule buttons.

**The problem is not the site. It is that Google is not surfacing the site.**

1. **The website is absent from page 1 of its own brand query** (`my appliance repair llc hamden`). Thumbtack, Facebook, Yelp, Nextdoor, MapQuest and Instagram all rank; `www.myappliance.us` does not. It is also absent from organic page 1 for `appliance repair hamden ct` and `appliance repair new haven ct`, while the GBP sits in the pack for both.
2. **Google's copy of the site is stale.** `site:www.myappliance.us` shows the homepage as "My Appliance Repair: Appliance Repair in Connecticut" and city pages as "Appliance Repair Branford, CT | Same-Day Service" with the old "Fast, reliable…" descriptions. Those titles were replaced on 2026-11-11. `/terms` and `/privacy` are still indexed although they have been `noindex` since the same change. Google has not recrawled the core pages in at least a week, and the September backlog of "Discovered – currently not indexed" was never confirmed cleared.
3. **Google folds the `site:` listing to 10 results** ("omitted some entries very similar"), which usually means a small indexed set, not 75.

Everything in the action plan below is ordered around getting Google to recrawl, trust and rank the site itself, because the GBP is already doing its job.

## Findings by category

### Indexing and crawl (Critical)

- **Search Console was not readable** (sign-in required). The repo's only GSC data is a June export filtered to the homepage. Without the Page indexing report, indexed-vs-excluded counts for the 75 URLs cannot be stated. This is the single most important data gap. Export **Pages → Indexing** and **Sitemaps** and drop them in `latest-google-performance/`.
- **Indexed titles are pre-Nov-11.** Evidence: Google's `site:` snippet for `/branford`, `/wallingford`, `/orange`, `/woodbridge` and `/`. Live titles differ. Googlebot has not fetched these since the retitle.
- **Noindex pages still indexed:** `/terms`, `/privacy`. Same cause. They will drop once recrawled.
- **Crawl demand is the likely cause, not errors.** All 90 crawled URLs responded correctly; TTFB 40–200 ms from Lighthouse, 110–1,000 ms from the raw crawl (cold-cache blog pages at the top end). Nothing server-side is slowing Googlebot.
- **Sitemap `lastmod`:** 35 of 75 URLs carry `2026-11-15`, the template-wide date, including city pages whose copy did not change that day. Google downgrades `lastmod` it finds unreliable. Keep the per-URL discipline the file already documents; do not bump a family unless its copy changed.
- Sitemap `priority`/`changefreq` are ignored by Google; harmless.

### On-page

- **Titles:** 21 city pages, 6 service pages, hubs: all unique, 49–67 chars. **Blog: 28 of 37 titles exceed 60 chars, 8 exceed 100, max 121** (`washing-machine-vibration-uneven-floors-connecticut`). Long titles get rewritten by Google.
- **Descriptions:** all present, 104–188 chars; city descriptions 119–138 (fine). Eight blog excerpts are over 160 and get truncated with "…".
- **H1s:** one per page. Blog index H1 "Expert Tips & Appliance Insights" carries no keyword. `/services/more-appliances` H1 reads "Specialty Appliances repair in Connecticut" while its title says "Freezer, Microwave & Cooktop Repair"; align them.
- **Homepage weight:** 6,240 words of visible text and 3,293 DOM nodes. The testimonial marquee renders 24 reviews twice (48 cards, 240 inline star SVGs) plus a second duplicated gallery. Google reads it fine, but it dilutes the page's topical focus and is the main reason mobile LCP is 3.3 s.
- **Internal linking:** city and service pages get 71 inbound links each (footer), which is good. **Blog posts get a median of 4, and 16 posts have exactly one** (the blog index). **32 of 37 post bodies contain no link to any service or city page**; the 37 posts together link to a service page 6 times and to a city page 0 times, but to `/booking` 80 times. The blog is not passing relevance to the money pages.
- **Breadcrumbs:** present with schema on city, service and blog pages.
- URL hygiene: `/Hamden` and `/HAMDEN` 404 (fine), `?utm` 200 with correct canonical (fine).

Add one verified job per city (the homepage already has a Naugatuck case; `RecentRepairs.tsx`) to make them stand on their own.
- **Blog:** 37 posts, 653–2,384 words (median 1,575), every post has H2s. Published Feb–Aug 2026; **nothing since 2026-08-07**, so the blog reads as abandoned. **Every post uses an Unsplash stock photo**, also as the Article `image`. ### Schema

- Site-wide graph: `HomeAndConstructionBusiness` + `WebSite` from the footer; `Service` + `BreadcrumbList` on city pages; `Service` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` on service pages; `Article` + `BreadcrumbList` on posts; `AboutPage`; `ItemList` on `/services`. All parse.

### Images

- 0 missing alt attributes across 90 pages. AVIF served. Responsive `srcset`/`sizes` on all `next/image` elements. Lazy loading on 31 of 34 homepage images.
- `/images/og-image.png` is **2.28 MB** (used as og:image on home, city, booking pages and as publisher logo). `/images/appliance-repair-connecticut-og.jpg` is 710 KB. Both should be under ~300 KB; large OG images are slow to fetch for social/AI crawlers and some scrapers skip them.
- Brand logos are inline SVG per card plus 240 star glyphs on the homepage. A single `<symbol>` sprite would cut ~200 KB of raw HTML.
